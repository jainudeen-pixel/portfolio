01 — About

Building the skills, not just the title.

I'm a third-year Computer Science student, and right now I'm a fresher in the truest sense — no security job title yet, just a structured, honest path toward one. My focus is offensive security: understanding how systems actually break so I can help people fix them ethically.

I'm currently on a 6-month roadmap toward the eJPT certification, working through networking fundamentals up through privilege escalation and Active Directory, and practicing everything on TryHackMe and Hack The Box rather than just reading about it.

Outside security, I run a print-on-demand shop on Etsy — which taught me SEO, positioning, and shipping something real to actual customers. Different domain, same instinct: figure out the system, then work it.
